WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

Mandla Mbatha School NPC respects the privacy of parents, guardians and website visitors. This notice explains how information submitted through this website is intended to be used. It is a draft operational notice and must be reviewed and approved by the school’s designated Information Officer before the website collects real personal information.

1. Information collected

The admission enquiry form asks for a parent or guardian’s name and telephone number, an optional email address, the learner’s name, the grade being enquired about and an optional message. The website does not request identity numbers, copies of identity documents, dates of birth or medical information.

2. Why the information is used

The details are intended to allow authorised school personnel to respond to the enquiry, provide information about the admission process and follow up where appropriate. Submitting an enquiry does not constitute an application decision or an offer of admission.

3. Storage and access

Enquiry records are stored in the website’s admissions database on the server selected by the school. Access is intended to be restricted to authorised administrators using a separate administrator key. The school must ensure that the hosting provider, backups, administrator accounts and downloaded records are protected appropriately.

4. AI school assistant

The optional school assistant sends the text of a visitor’s general question to the OpenAI API to generate a response. It is not connected to the admissions database. Do not enter a learner’s private information, identity numbers, contact details, medical details or documents into the assistant. OpenAI API processing is subject to the applicable OpenAI API terms and data controls.

5. Sharing and retention

The school should not disclose enquiry information except where authorised, necessary to provide the requested service, or required by law. The school must approve and publish a retention schedule, and securely delete or de-identify records when they are no longer required.

6. Your choices and information requests

You may contact the school to ask about the personal information submitted through this website, request correction of inaccurate details, or raise a privacy concern. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and verification procedures.
