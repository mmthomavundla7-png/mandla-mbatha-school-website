GET TO KNOW US

Discover Mandla Mbatha School NPC

Mandla Mbatha School NPC is an independent learning institution in Tembisa, Gauteng, committed to providing a supportive, academically focused environment.

We believe education is the foundation of knowledge, discipline, leadership and lifelong learning. Our slogan is We Educate to Educate.

Our Vision

To empower learners through education and prepare them to contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To promote quality teaching, academic development, discipline and personal growth.

Our Values

Respect, integrity, responsibility, excellence, teamwork and leadership.

ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE

Our Academic Programme

Our programme is planned to support learners through their educational journey.

Foundation Phase

Grade RR – Grade 3

Building literacy, numeracy and essential learning foundations.

Intermediate Phase

Grade 4 – Grade 6

Developing knowledge, communication and independent learning.

Senior Grades

Grade 7 – Grade 12

Preparing learners for further education and future opportunities.

Language of Learning and Teaching: English. Curriculum: CAPS-aligned, subject to the school's approved offering and registration status.
